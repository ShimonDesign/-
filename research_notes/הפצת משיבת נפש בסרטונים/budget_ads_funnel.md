# Paid promotion, budgeting and sales funnel for "משיבת נפש" via short videos (Israel, 2026)

Method note: Research date 2026-11-02. Only web search snippets were usable. WebFetch could not reach several hosts from this session (superads.ai, a-2-z.co.il, ads.tiktok.com failed DNS), so most figures come from search-result summaries of those pages, not from reading them in full. FX used for conversions: about 3.05 ILS per USD (moneyexchangerate.org, 2026-10-04: 1 USD = 3.05 ILS; 1 Oct 2026 range 3.01 to 3.09) — [moneyexchangerate.org](https://moneyexchangerate.org/currencyexchange/usd/ils/1250). Treat every benchmark below as a planning range, not a quote.

## 1. Ad costs in Israel (Meta Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts), minimum budgets, religious-content restrictions, boosting organic winners

### Takeaway
Israel is a cheap Meta market. Vendor data puts all-industry Facebook CPM at about $6.5 to $10.2 (about 20 to 31 ILS) and CPC at about $0.55 to $0.58 (about 1.7 to 1.8 ILS), roughly half the global level. Video-view metrics (ThruPlay, Shorts CPV) are only available as global vendor ranges. The main policy risk is targeting rather than content. Meta removed religion-based detailed targeting in 2022. TikTok bans targeting by religion, and one TikTok help page reads as a ban on religious content in ads. TikTok paid ads are therefore the riskiest channel for this book.

### Cited Findings
**Meta (Facebook/Instagram) CPM/CPC, Israel**
- Israel all-industry Facebook CPM by month: Nov 2024 $8.9, Dec 2024 $6.5, Jan 2025 $8.4, Feb 2025 $10.2 (peak), Mar 2025 $9.3. Described as "well below the global benchmark yet move with sharper swings" — [Superads Israel CPM](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cpm-cost-per-mille/israel) (via search snippet, page not fetchable)
- Israel Entertainment-category CPM averaged $8.88 across 2025 ($6.99 in Jan, $9.82 in Dec), against a $20.15 global average — [Superads Entertainment Israel](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cpm-cost-per-mille/entertainment/israel)
- Israel fitness and training centers CPM averaged 6.19 over 12 months, with spikes in May (9.51) and November (8.75) — [Superads](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cpm-cost-per-mille/fitness-training-centers/israel)
- Israel all-industry median Facebook CPC is about $0.55 to $0.58, against about $1.07 to $1.14 globally — [Superads Israel CPC](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cpc-cost-per-click/israel). Superads does not document its methodology, and date windows differ between pages.
- Superads also has an Israel cost-per-purchase page, but its figures were not visible in the snippets — [Superads cost per purchase Israel](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cost-per-purchase/israel)
- Global reference averages: Databox CPM $5.61; Neal Schaffer CPC $0.62, CPM $11.62, CPA $18.68; WordStream 2025 traffic-campaign CPC $0.70 — [Strike Social / aggregated search](https://strikesocial.com/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-advertise-on-facebook/); [Neal Schaffer](https://nealschaffer.com/facebook-ads-cost/); [WordStream 2025](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/facebook-ads-benchmarks-2025)
- Cost per ThruPlay, by industry (global, one vendor, no methodology): gaming $0.01 to $0.04, real estate $0.05 to $0.12, SaaS/B2B $0.06 to $0.15, finance $0.08 to $0.15 — [Adligator 2026](https://adligator.com/ru/blog/facebook-thruplay-benchmarks-po-industriyam-2026)
- Instagram generic ranges (single, low-quality source): CPC $0.35 to $3.00, CPM $5 to $30, "minimum daily budget around $25" for small businesses. That minimum is a recommendation, not Meta policy — [thepricer.org](https://thepricer.org/how-much-do-instagram-ads-cost)

**Meta minimum budgets**
- Meta's minimum varies by currency, objective, bid strategy and schedule. The exact local-currency minimum is shown in the ad set's daily budget field in Ads Manager. No source published an ILS figure — [tryvizup 2026](https://www.tryvizup.com/blog/meta-ads-minimum-budget-requirements-2026); [cropink Mar 2026](https://cropink.com/minimum-budget-for-facebook-ads)
- Third-party claim: the platform floor is about $1/day, and "functional" awareness minimums are about $10 to $15/day — [cropink](https://cropink.com/minimum-budget-for-facebook-ads)
- Brazil example (an analogue, not Israel): R$3/day for reach, traffic or engagement; R$6/day for leads, messages or conversions; R$10/day for ThruPlay — [podvector](https://podvector.ai/articles/ads/scaling-and-diagnosis/meta-ads-minimum-daily-budget-brazil-2026)
- Instagram's in-app "Promote" flow shows the final price and estimated reach before you confirm — [ice.co.il guide](https://www.ice.co.il/advertising-marketing/news/article/832131)

**TikTok**
- No Israel-specific TikTok CPM data was found. Global ranges: In-Feed CPM about $4 to $8; other sources give $10 to $30. A 2026 index cited by Trendtrack puts TikTok's average CPM at $3.50 against Meta's $6.59. Spark Ads CPM is about $5 to $10 — [Trendtrack](https://www.trendtrack.io/it/blog-post/quanto-costa-una-campagna-tiktok-ads); [Darkroom 2025](https://www.darkroomagency.com/observatory/how-much-does-tiktok-advertising-cost-in-2025); [affninja 2026](https://affninja.com/tiktok-ads-pricing/)
- TikTok Ads Manager minimums, as reported by third parties: $50/day per campaign and $20/day per ad group. Sources conflict on a "$500 minimum campaign budget" — [magicbrief](https://magicbrief.com/post/how-much-do-tiktok-ads-cost-understanding-the-budget-for-advertising-on-tiktok); [foreplay](https://foreplay.co/post/how-much-do-tiktok-ads-cost-per-day)
- TikTok ads policy ("Politics, Religion, and Culture"): ads may not disrespect religious elements, cultures or figures, and may not include hateful content — [TikTok Ads policy](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-ads-policy-politics-religion-and-culture?lang=en)
- TikTok's minors-protection advertising page says ads with "political, religious, or other sensitive content are not permitted". This is the broadest statement found, and it sits oddly on that page — [TikTok protecting minors](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/protecting-minors-on-tiktok-advertising-initiatives)
- TikTok prohibits targeting by protected characteristics, including religion. Its Inventory Filter keeps ads away from content that disrespects religion — [TikTok inventory filter](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-inventory-filter); [lseo](https://lseo.com/paid-media/paid-social-media-marketing/an-insider-look-understanding-tiktoks-advertising-policies/)

**Meta religious targeting**
- Since 19 Jan 2022, Meta has removed Detailed Targeting options for "sensitive" topics, including causes, organizations or public figures tied to religion. Advertisers can still target in other ways. The change applies to Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and the Audience Network — [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/news/meta-to-remove-ads-that-target-religion-and-politics-from-2022); [NBC](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/amp/rcna5029); [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2021/11/09/meta-facebook-advertising-block?stream=technology)
- No source found says Meta classifies religious-book ads as a "Special Ad Category". Those categories are housing, employment, credit and social issues/elections/politics.

**YouTube Shorts**
- Average Shorts ads CPV is $0.10 to $0.30 (Strike Social, Q1 2025). That is higher than in-stream CPV (about $0.026 to $0.05), but Shorts CPM is lower, at about $4 (Precise.TV, 2024) — [Store Growers](https://www.storegrowers.com/youtube-ads-benchmarks/); [Tinuiti](https://tinuiti.com/blog/paid-social/10-youtube-trends/); [topgrowthmarketing 2026](https://topgrowthmarketing.com/youtube-ads-cost/)
- Under CPV bidding, you don't pay if a viewer skips before the threshold — [Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2472735)

**Boosting organic winners / Meta tools**
- Meta's Partnership Ads Hub in Ads Manager recommends existing organic creator or brand content likely to perform as paid ads, now on Facebook as well. Partnership ads can show a single creator in the header, and the brand handle is optional — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-announces-threads-video-ads-creator-collaborations/747619/); [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/meta-amplifies-reels-with-new-advertising-features)
- "Reels trending ads" place ads right after popular creator Reels. Meta was testing them in May 2025 — [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/413917/); [Media in Canada](https://mediaincanada.com/2025/05/09/newfronts-2025-meta)
- Meta recommends 9:16 vertical video for Reels ads — [Website Builder Expert](https://www.websitebuilderexpert.com/news/meta-updates-reels-ads-guide/)

### Inferences
- In ILS at about 3.05/USD: Meta CPM in Israel is about 20 to 31 ILS and CPC about 1.7 to 1.8 ILS (all-industry median). A cheap engagement/ThruPlay campaign in a niche, Hebrew-language audience could plausibly reach about 0.10 to 0.30 ILS per ThruPlay. That is an extrapolation from the global $0.01 to $0.15 range and Israel's below-average CPM. Validate it with a 7-day test of 30 to 50 ILS/day.
- Primary channel: Meta (Reels plus Facebook, where Israel's Haredi and traditional older audience is strong). Use organic TikTok and Spark-style amplification only cautiously, because of TikTok's religious-content language and its $50/day campaign minimum, which is about 150 ILS/day. YouTube Shorts ads are cost-inefficient per view (about 0.30 to 0.90 ILS CPV), but they are useful for a long-form YouTube channel.
- Since religion interests can't be targeted on Meta, target with Hebrew language, Israel geo, broad/Advantage+ audiences, custom audiences (video viewers at 50%/95%, page/IG engagers, WhatsApp list, site visitors), and lookalikes built from buyers. The creative itself (Rabbi Nachman quotes, Breslov imagery) does the audience selection.
- Testing rule: post organically, then boost only the top 10 to 20% by 3-second hold and retention, after 48 to 72 hours. Run 3 to 5 days at 30 to 50 ILS/day each, and keep those under target cost per ThruPlay or cost per landing-page view. This is standard practice; the 10 to 20% figure is a heuristic, not a sourced rule.

### Gaps
- No Israel-specific ThruPlay, TikTok or YouTube Shorts cost data was found. No ILS minimum daily budget was published for Meta or TikTok.
- Could not fetch the official TikTok policy page to confirm whether a non-disrespectful religious-book ad is allowed in Israel. Recommend testing one ad through review, or asking TikTok support.
- No 2026 Meta documentation found on retargeting video viewers. It is a long-standing feature, but this was not verified this session.

## 2. Sales funnel for a book in Israel: e-commerce, payment providers, WhatsApp, shipping, printing, the "הפצה"/donor model, digital editions

### Takeaway
A lean Israeli book funnel costs little in fixed fees. Grow (formerly Meshulam) has a no-monthly-fee plan at about 1.4% + 1 ILS per transaction. Israel Post registered mail is about 13 to 27 ILS. WhatsApp marketing messages are about $0.035 (about 0.11 ILS) each. The culturally strongest model in Breslov is the non-profit "מחיר הפצה" (distribution price) and donor-funded printing fund. The "מלא וגדיש" association and the Odesser publishing house are existing examples.

### Cited Findings
**Payments**
- Grow (Meshulam): charges a fee only on completed transactions, with no fixed monthly payment. Up to 5,000 ILS/month: 1.4% + VAT + 1 ILS per transaction, plus 0.5% extra for Diners cards — [JGive help (Grow fees)](https://intercom.help/jgive-support/en/articles/11931888)
- Grow via Morning (Green Invoice) has tiers:
  - Extra+Prime: no monthly fee. 1.4% up to 5,000 ILS/month, 1.2% for 5,001 to 20,000, 1% for 20,001 to 150,000, 0.95% above that.
  - Basic: 29 ILS/month + 1.6%.
  - Both add about 1.2 ILS per transaction. Chargeback fee is 40 ILS; cap is 20,000 ILS per transaction.
  - Source: [Morning help center](https://www.greeninvoice.co.il/help-center/dp-terms/)
- Cardcom: no official price list found. Third-party estimates conflict: about 1.2 to 1.4% per transaction with plans from about 59 ILS/month for the invoicing add-on, versus 0.6 to 0.8%. Pricing is negotiated per merchant — [skillselion](https://skillselion.com/skills/skills-il/tax-and-finance/cardcom-payment-gateway)
- The Bank of Israel announced a cut of about 30% to the interchange fee on deferred-debit cards, from 0.7% to 0.5% in three steps — [Bank of Israel](https://boi.org.il/publications/pressreleases/העמלה-הצולבת-תופחת-בכ-30-משיעור-של-07-לשיעור-של-05-ב-3-פעימות/)

**Shipping**
- Israel Post registered mail costs about 16.30 ILS including SMS, or 13.30 ILS when prepared and paid online (date unclear). Fast registered mail (48 hours) went from 25 to 27 ILS. In January 2025 most private-customer tariffs changed only for VAT — [bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/general/news/article/20012214); [calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/local_news/article/h1ohyaq81x)
- One report says domestic parcel flat rates rose about 25%, to 24 to 45 ILS per parcel up to 20 kg (date unclear) — [calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/local_news/article/byzdrad00s)
- Israel Post became a private company in November 2024. It can set its own prices except for stamps, "דואר 72" and registered mail, which stay regulated — [ice.co.il](https://www.ice.co.il/consumerism/news/article/918670)
- Couriers and pickup points (Get Package, UPS Israel and others): no published small-business rates were found. Get Package reports January, March and September as peak months, with about 20% more shipments — [ice.co.il](https://www.ice.co.il/consumerism/news/article/1091833); [walla](https://finance.walla.co.il/item/3731084)

**WhatsApp**
- Since 1 July 2025, the WhatsApp Business Platform charges per delivered template message. Israel's marketing rate is about $0.0353 per message; utility and authentication are about $0.0053. Service replies are free. A BSP platform fee comes on top — [Meta developer docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing); [aggregator table, sent.dm](https://docs.sent.dm/docs/guides/whatsapp-pricing)

**Breslov distribution culture ("הפצה")**
- The "מלא וגדיש" association sells Rabbi Nachman's books at non-profit "distribution prices" so that people in hard financial situations can own them. Its model relies on a printing fund renewed by donations. The source is mostly the body's own marketing — [Kipa](https://www.kipa.co.il/כדאי-לדעת/1209763-0/); [JDN](https://www.jdn.co.il/consumer/2466289/)
- Rabbi Yisrael Dov Odesser spent his life printing and distributing Rabbi Nachman's books, raising donations worldwide. A publishing house founded with those funds is now run by his grandson, Rabbi Amram Horowitz — [HaMichlol](https://www.hamichlol.org.il/רבי_ישראל_דב_אודסר); [Hidabroot](https://www.hidabroot.org/article/1215698)
- Another article on the distribution of Breslov books ("מנגישים את האור") — [Kikar](https://www.kikar.co.il/333/t1uqta)

**Printing**
- No per-copy prices for short-run or print-on-demand book printing in Israel were found. Digital-print shops exist (D.co.il listings), and some don't handle small quantities — [d.co.il](https://www.d.co.il/h-c12390-e1-p0-l0/)

### Inferences
- Lean stack:
  - WooCommerce on cheap hosting, or a simple landing page, plus Grow or Morning payment links with no monthly fee.
  - A WhatsApp Business app (free) for small volume. Move to the API only for broadcasts: 1,000 marketing messages cost about 108 ILS.
  - Israel Post registered mail at about 13 to 16 ILS, or pickup-point couriers (quotes required).
- Paid ads alone rarely pay for a low-price book. A funnel built on "sponsor a distribution" fits Breslov culture well, with tiers such as 18 / 36 / 180 / 1,800 ILS ("חי" multiples). Donors fund free or discounted copies, and the videos solicit both readers and sponsors, which turns ad spend into a donation channel. The tier amounts are conventional Jewish donation multiples, not sourced figures.
- If donations are solicited, using a registered עמותה (Section 46 tax receipts) through JGive or Grow donation pages improves conversion. JGive's help center lists Grow fees, which suggests that integration exists.
- A free PDF or audiobook in exchange for a WhatsApp opt-in is a cheap lead magnet. Each subscriber later costs about 0.11 ILS per marketing broadcast.

### Gaps
- No confirmed 2026 Israel Post per-parcel price for a book of about 300 to 500 g. No courier or pickup-point rate cards. No Shopify, WooCommerce or Tranzila prices retrieved.
- No Israeli print-on-demand price per copy (local quotes are needed: page count, size, binding, quantity).
- No public data on Odesser-fund distribution volumes or donor economics.

## 3. Freelancer and creator costs (video editors, Fiverr, AI tools, Breslov/Jewish micro-influencers)

### Takeaway
Only international rates were found. Reels editing runs about $40 to $150 per short (about 120 to 460 ILS), with very low-end Fiverr offers from $5. No Israeli or Breslov-niche influencer rate cards were found.

### Cited Findings
- A freelancer job paid $150 per Reel for 50 Reels of 30 to 50 seconds with color grading, music and on-screen text — [Freelancer](https://www.freelancer.de/projects/animation/cinematic-short-film-reel-creation)
- One editor's intro offer: $250 for a first batch of 6 shorts, about $42 each. Other offers start at $5 — [Contra](https://contra.com/s/FvQesZZ6-cinematic-short-form-video-editing-reelstik-toks-and-shorts); [Contra](https://contra.com/s/OslmNrek-professional-short-form-video-editor-for-reels-and-tik-toks)
- CapCut's 2025 guide gives freelance editor package ranges: Starter $300 to $650, Pro $900 to $1,800, Launch $2,000 to $4,500 — [CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/resource/freelance-video-editors)

### Inferences
- Budget planning:
  - DIY with CapCut or AI subtitles: about 0 to 100 ILS/month in tools.
  - Offshore or Fiverr editor: about 50 to 150 ILS per short.
  - Israeli Hebrew-native editor: likely about 150 to 400 ILS per short. This is unverified; check Israeli Facebook freelancer groups.
- For 3 months at 3 to 5 shorts/week (about 40 to 60 shorts), editing would cost about 2,000 to 9,000 ILS outsourced.
- For the Breslov niche, collaboration or barter with existing Breslov WhatsApp channels, Instagram/TikTok accounts and synagogue networks (free copies, a dedication) is likely cheaper than paid influencers. Meta Partnership ads let you boost a creator's post directly.

### Gaps
- No Israeli influencer price list was found, either general or for the religious/Breslov niche. The search returned unrelated CPI results.
- No Israeli editor price list in ILS.

## 4. Benchmarks and case studies: expected reach and sales; Israeli BookTok; 3-month budget tiers

### Takeaway
No Israeli case study with hard numbers for book promotion via Reels/TikTok, or for Jewish content campaigns, was found. Israeli BookTok exists but is centered on romance. Expected results have to be modeled from the CPM/CPC figures above, so the tiers below are inference.

### Cited Findings
- An Israel Hayom opinion piece says there is an active Israeli BookTok community, but it revolves around romance novels, with crowds of young women at the book fair's romance stalls. This is opinion, not sales data — [Israel Hayom](https://www.israelhayom.co.il/opinions/article/20727842?amp=1)
- Global: BookTok-driven adult fiction grew 25% in 2021 and another 8% the following year (PBS). Retailers added "BookTok made me buy it" sections — [PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/show/how-booktok-is-giving-authors-and-booksellers-a-much-needed-boost); [CTV](https://www.ctvnews.ca/business/article/popular-tiktok-videos-with-booktok-hashtag-give-book-retailer-indigo-a-sales-boost/)
- Israel CPM is about $6.5 to $10.2; CPC is about $0.55 to $0.58 — [Superads](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cpm-cost-per-mille/israel)

### Inferences (3-month budget model, ILS, assumptions stated)
Assumptions:
- Meta CPM 25 ILS, landing-page CPC about 2 to 3 ILS (niche audience, conversion objective above the median).
- Landing page to purchase about 1.5 to 3% for a 40 to 70 ILS book.
- Cost per purchase therefore about 70 to 200 ILS. This is above the book price, so paid ads mostly buy reach, list growth and donors, not profitable direct sales.

| Item (3 months) | Lean | Recommended | Aggressive |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meta boosts / ads | 1,500 (about 17 ILS/day; boost only 2 to 3 winners/month) | 6,000 (about 65 ILS/day: test plus scale plus retargeting) | 18,000 (about 200 ILS/day) |
| TikTok / YouTube Shorts ads | 0 (organic only) | 1,000 (Shorts or Spark test, if TikTok policy allows) | 4,500 (TikTok at about 150 ILS/day for 30 days, or Shorts) |
| Video production | 0 to 300 (DIY, CapCut/AI tools) | 3,000 to 6,000 (about 30 to 40 shorts at 100 to 150 ILS) | 10,000 to 15,000 (about 50 to 60 shorts by an Israeli editor plus a filming day) |
| Creators / collabs | 0 (barter: free copies) | 1,500 (2 to 4 niche creators or Partnership ads) | 6,000 |
| Funnel tools | about 0 to 300 (Grow/Morning with no monthly fee, WhatsApp app, a landing page) | about 600 to 1,000 (WooCommerce hosting, domain, WA API about 100 to 300) | about 1,500 to 2,500 (Shopify-type stack, CRM, WA broadcasts) |
| Fulfilment (per order, not budget) | about 15 to 30 ILS per copy shipping, plus about 1.4% + 1 ILS payment fee | same | same |
| **Total marketing (excl. print and shipping)** | **about 1,500 to 2,100** | **about 12,000 to 15,500** | **about 40,000 to 51,000** |

Expected outcomes per tier (rough):
- Lean: about 60k to 100k paid impressions plus organic reach.
- Recommended: about 250k to 300k paid impressions, about 2,000 to 3,000 link clicks, about 40 to 100 paid-attributed sales or sponsorships.
- Aggressive: about 800k to 1M impressions (Israel's Hebrew-speaking religious audience saturates, so frequency rises), about 150 to 350 sales or sponsorships.

These are model outputs, not benchmarks. Organic virality and the donor/"sponsor a הפצה" model can change the economics far more than ad spend. One sponsor at 180 to 1,800 ILS can cover dozens of copies.

### Gaps
- No Israeli case studies with metrics on book promotion via Reels/TikTok, or on Jewish/Breslov content campaigns (for example, Breslov organizations' ad results).
- No conversion-rate benchmarks for Israeli book e-commerce or Hebrew religious content.
- Superads Israel cost-per-purchase figures could not be read because the page was not fetchable. If the coordinator can allow superads.ai, a-2-z.co.il and ads.tiktok.com, re-fetching would firm up the numbers.
