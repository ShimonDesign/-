# Short-Video Algorithms (2025–2026) and Distribution Channels for an Israeli Hebrew Audience: Breslov / "משיבת נפש" Short Videos

Research note: 16 tool calls. Israeli news hosts (maariv.co.il, israelhayom.co.il) and tubefilter.com could not be fetched from this session because the network allowlist blocks them. Israeli figures below therefore come from search-result summaries of those articles, not full-text reads. Check the exact numbers against the original Israel Internet Association (איגוד האינטרנט הישראלי) index before using them in the final report.

## 1. Platform ranking signals and distribution mechanics (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels)

### Takeaway
Every platform now ranks short videos on how well they hold a viewer: whether people stop scrolling, how long they watch, and whether they rewatch. Instagram explicitly treats "sends" (DM shares) as the top signal for reaching non-followers, and Meta (Instagram and Facebook) penalizes unoriginal or reposted content harder than before. Since April 2026 Instagram judges originality across an account's whole month of posts. This favors short, originally filmed clips that people will want to send to a friend, a natural fit for spiritual "חיזוק" (encouragement) content.

### Cited Findings
**Instagram (Adam Mosseri statements)**
- In January 2025 Mosseri said: "The top three signals that matter most for ranking are watch time, likes and sends." He told creators to track *average watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach* rather than raw totals. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/); [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-reveals-key-factors-for-boosting-reach-8646916)
- Sends matter more for reaching new people (unconnected reach) and likes matter more with existing followers. Mosseri defined sends as how many viewers "sent it to a friend in a DM, out of everyone who saw the post." The often-repeated claim that a send is worth "several times a like" is blog paraphrase, not a Mosseri figure. — [Search Engine Journal](https://searchenginejournal.com/instagram-algorithm-shift-why-sends-matter-more-than-ever/521389); [kompozy.io](https://kompozy.io/news/instagram-mosseri-ranking-signals-guidance)
- Reels watch time is reportedly measured both as a percentage of the video watched and as absolute seconds. This is a secondary source. — [dataslayer.ai](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers)
- **Trial Reels** (official): a Reel is shown first only to non-followers, and the creator can then decide whether to share it to followers. Global rollout followed testing that began in May 2024. Metrics are available after about 24 hours (some sources say 24–72 hours). Followers can still see a Trial Reel if a non-follower DMs it to them or it surfaces in search by audio, location or filter. A third-party guide says access was expanded in mid-2025 to creators with 1,000+ followers. This is not confirmed by Instagram. — [Instagram Creators blog](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels); [BetaNews, July 2025](https://betanews.com/2025/07/14/instagram-opens-up-trial-reels-feature-to-more-creators/); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/social-media/instagram-will-let-video-creators-make-trial-reels-for-non-followers-160016725.html)
- **Originality, April 30 2026:** the originality rules that covered Reels since 2024 now also cover photos and carousels. Instagram evaluates *what an account publishes over a month*, not single posts. Repost-heavy aggregator accounts are removed from recommendations, reposts are replaced with the original, and labels are added. Following an aggregator still shows its content to followers. Mosseri says reposting is acceptable if the post is "meaningfully transformed": voiceover, green screen, your own text overlay, commentary, or a Collab with the original creator. The trigger threshold reported in trade press, about 10+ reposts in 30 days, is not in Instagram's own wording. — [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/); [Tubefilter](https://tubefilter.com/2026/04/30/instagram-removes-algorithm-recommendations-repost-content-aggregator); [Lindsey Gamble](https://lindseygamble.com/blog/instagram-is-tweaking-its-algorithm-to-prioritize-original-content-creators)

**YouTube Shorts**
- The official Creator Insider interview with Shorts product lead Todd Sherman is the main primary source. Shorts are ranked differently from long-form, and the key comparative metric is **"viewed vs. swiped away."** YouTube does not publish thresholds. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-explains-how-shorts-algorithm-works/494953/); [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)
- A non-YouTube estimate from a strategist's analysis of 3.3B Shorts found the best performers had a 70–90% "viewed" rate. Third-party sources describe seed-audience testing, in which a Short is shown to a small group first and pushed further only if retention is strong. YouTube has not stated this. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/youtube-shorts-algorithm/); [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)
- From March 31 2025, a Shorts "view" counts any start or replay with no minimum watch time. The old metric was renamed **"engaged views"** (under Advanced Mode in Analytics), and monetization and YPP eligibility still use engaged views. — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-changes-how-shorts-views-are-counted-from-march-31/); [Sprout Social support](https://support.sproutsocial.com/hc/en-us/articles/35874991211533-YouTube-Shorts-View-Count-Update-March-2025)

**TikTok**
- Several 2026 third-party guides say TikTok now tests new videos with existing followers first and then a wider audience. Search is a growing discovery channel, longer videos are rewarded, and content diversity is weighted. None of this was confirmed against the TikTok Newsroom in this research. — [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/tiktok-algorithm/); [mickyweis.com](https://www.mickyweis.com/en/tiktok-algorithm-2026)
- In summer 2025 TikTok launched user controls for the For You feed: "Manage Topics" (see more or less of a category) and Smart Keyword Filters. — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/tiktok-algorithm)
- US ownership restructuring was reported, with a January 22 2026 agreement to divest US operations to an Oracle/Silver Lake/MGX-led group and the US algorithm reportedly being retrained. This comes from single blog sources and is likely irrelevant to Israeli users, who remain on global TikTok. — [socialpilot](https://socialpilot.co/blog/tiktok-algorithm)

**Facebook Reels**
- A leaked 2025 memo from Facebook head Tom Alison set goals to "make Reels successful," "build world-class recommendations technology," and "unlock messaging-based sharing." In October 2025 Meta rolled out a new Reels recommendation engine that continues promoting original content, with plans for one model to power the whole video ecosystem. — [Tubefilter, Oct 2025](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/10/09/meta-facebook-reels-recommendation-algorithm-update/); [ChannelNews](https://www.channelnews.com.au/facebook-changing-algorithm-to-mimic-tiktok-leak-reveals/)
- Secondary sources describe a March 2026 originality push on Facebook. Accounts that repeatedly post unoriginal content can lose recommendations, a voiceover over someone else's clip alone may *not* count as original, and third-party clips can qualify if they add meaningful value. — [SocialPilot](https://socialpilot.co/blog/facebook-algorithm); [Virlo](https://virlo.ai/blog/facebook-algorithm)

### Inferences
- The best single metric to engineer is the share or DM-forward on Instagram, which maps directly onto Israeli WhatsApp forwarding culture (see section 3). Each video should end with a reason to send it ("שלח למישהו שצריך את זה היום", "send this to someone who needs it today").
- The first 1–2 seconds decide "viewed vs. swiped away" on Shorts and the hold rate on Reels and TikTok. Open with the punchline or quote from משיבת נפש on screen, not a logo or intro.
- Under the April 2026 rules, the account must film its own footage or heavily transform anything else. Reposting other Breslov rabbis' clips, or clips from Breslov channels, would endanger recommendations for the *whole account*.
- Use Trial Reels as a free A/B test of hooks with non-followers before publishing to followers. This fits a new brand with few followers once it passes any follower threshold.
- AI-generated visuals or voice would need labeling. See Gaps.

### Gaps
- No primary Mosseri video, TikTok Newsroom page, or YouTube Creator Insider transcript was fetched. All platform statements come from trade press quoting them.
- **AI-content labeling rules** (TikTok AIGC label, Meta "AI info", YouTube altered/synthetic disclosure) were not covered by these searches. Verify them separately.
- **Watermark penalties**: no 2025–2026 source was found confirming that Instagram demotes TikTok-watermarked videos, beyond the general originality rules. Earlier Instagram statements (2021) said this, but they were not re-verified here.
- **Posting frequency, best times, hashtags vs. keyword SEO, captions**: no authoritative 2025–2026 source was collected. Treat any numbers as untested.

## 2. Israel specifics: platform usage by sector, WhatsApp, Telegram, kosher ecosystem

### Takeaway
WhatsApp reaches almost every Israeli (about 98–99%) and is the dominant channel for the Haredi sector, which uses it far more for news and content groups than open social media. Instagram (about 82% reach in 2026 per the Israel Internet Association index) and YouTube are the broadest video platforms for the secular and traditional public. TikTok reach is high but daily use is falling (34% daily in 2026). Kosher phones and filters (נטפרי, kosher lines, the Rabbinical Committee for Communications) cut off most of the Haredi core from video platforms, so they are reached through WhatsApp, audio lines and filtered sites.

### Cited Findings
- **Israel Internet Association index, 2025 data (reported):** WhatsApp is the most used digital platform at 99%. Daily use: Instagram 67%, TikTok 55%, Telegram 44%. X is at 32%, down 5 points. One summary cites TikTok and Telegram reach as 71% and 57%, but the ordering in the source text was ambiguous. — [Maariv summary](https://www.maariv.co.il/economy/tech/article-1245210); [Israel Hayom](https://www.israelhayom.co.il/tech/tech-news/article/20630679)
- **Index, 2026 data (reported):** Instagram 82% (+1 point), TikTok 59% reach but only **34% daily**, Telegram down 4 points year on year. The same coverage calls Facebook "a network of old people." The TikTok reach figures conflict between reports (59% vs 71%), so verify against the original index. — [Maariv, 2026](https://www.maariv.co.il/economy/tech/article-1326023); [Maariv, 2025](https://www.maariv.co.il/economy/tech/article-1245210)
- **First index edition, 2023:** YouTube 97%, WhatsApp 95%, Facebook 90%, Instagram 70%, TikTok about 50%. — [ESRA Magazine](https://magazine.esra.org.il/posts/entry/the-internet.html); [JPost](https://m.jpost.com/israel-news/article-742457)
- Globes ranked WhatsApp Israel's top brand for 2024: 98% reach, and 95% of users open it daily. — [Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-1001484162)
- **Haredi sector:** a Bezeq/Kantar survey of 2022 data found 54% of Haredi internet users active on WhatsApp, 69% of them using it for non-personal updates such as news groups. Haredi influencers work mainly on WhatsApp, while the general public uses Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. — [JDN](https://www.jdn.co.il/j_world/2474197/)
- An Israel Democracy Institute study (via Algemeiner, undated) found 46% of Haredim use WhatsApp vs 97% of other Jews, and 88% of Haredi parents restrict their children's online access. Haredi internet use more than doubled since 2008. — [Algemeiner](https://www.algemeiner.com/?p=600443)
- WhatsApp blocked third-party bots that let Haredi influencers mass-post Status updates to tens of thousands of contacts, with some 21-day bans. Influencers moved to costlier tools or manual posting. — [JDN](https://www.jdn.co.il/j_world/2474197/)
- Kosher lines cannot access the internet, so **voice-WhatsApp-style audio groups** are widespread among Haredim. The Rabbinical Committee for Communications reportedly can monitor and block voice services on kosher lines, and this was challenged in court in 2026. — [Bhol](https://www.bhol.co.il/news/1709327); [Bhol](https://www.bhol.co.il//news/1091420)
- Kosher WhatsApp variants exist, and some kosher-phone providers moved customers to a restricted "red" kosher WhatsApp that limits which groups and content can be reached. — [Bhol](https://www.bhol.co.il/news/766426); [Bhol](https://www.bhol.co.il/news/162435)
- Netfree filtering shapes which Torah sites are reachable. For example, a Haredi forum was founded after Netfree blocked its predecessor. — [Hamichlol](https://www.hamichlol.org.il/חרדים_לדעת)
- The Breslov Research Institute runs a WhatsApp group that sends daily short encouragement posts and clips, a precedent for Breslov content via WhatsApp. — [Breslov.org](https://breslov.org/the-breslov-research-institute-whatsapp-group/)

### Inferences
- **Secular and traditional (חילוני/מסורתי) "seekers"**: best reached on Instagram Reels, which has the broadest daily reach and the sends signal, plus YouTube Shorts and TikTok. Facebook Reels and Facebook groups mainly reach older traditional audiences (40+).
- **Dati Leumi (דתי לאומי) and Breslov-adjacent audiences**: Instagram, WhatsApp groups and Status, and Telegram channels.
- **Haredi audience**: largely unreachable through video apps. Use WhatsApp Channels and groups, a short audio version for kosher-line audio lines, and Haredi sites. Video files on WhatsApp may be blocked on red/kosher WhatsApp, so consider audio and text-plus-image versions.
- Falling TikTok daily use (34%) suggests treating TikTok as a secondary cross-post rather than the main bet.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 breakdown of platform use by religiosity (secular/traditional/religious/Haredi) was found. The Taboola early-2025 survey sampled Haredi and Arab respondents, but no sector breakdown was visible.
- DataReportal/Meltwater Israel 2025/2026 and the Bezeq Internet Report 2025 were not retrieved. Their user totals are missing.
- No data was found on WhatsApp Channels subscriber sizes in Israel, on Israeli religious content sites' traffic (e.g., Hidabroot, Breslev Israel, Kikar), or on which platform has the most reach for Jewish/spiritual content specifically.

## 3. Engineering sharing, collaborations, cross-posting workflow; account setup and funnel; measurement

### Takeaway
Sharing is both the strongest algorithm signal (Instagram sends) and the native Israeli behavior (WhatsApp forwards to about 98% of the population). The plan should build "forwardable" clips, seed them into existing WhatsApp and Telegram communities, and use Instagram Collabs with original creators, which Mosseri names as an acceptable originality path. Cross-posting the same original file is fine if it is clean and unwatermarked, but reposting *other people's* content is penalized at the account level.

### Cited Findings
- Mosseri's listed ways to stay "original" when using others' material include a Collab with the original creator, your own voiceover, commentary, text, or green screen. — [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/)
- Meta's Facebook strategy explicitly aims to "unlock messaging-based sharing." — [ChannelNews](https://www.channelnews.com.au/facebook-changing-algorithm-to-mimic-tiktok-leak-reveals/)
- Instagram insight metrics to track weekly, per Mosseri: average watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/)
- On YouTube Shorts, track "engaged views" and "viewed vs. swiped away" rather than the inflated post-March-2025 view count. Benchmark from a third-party analysis: 70–90% viewed. — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-changes-how-shorts-views-are-counted-from-march-31/); [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)
- Mass-automated WhatsApp Status posting via bots risks WhatsApp bans of about 21 days. — [JDN](https://www.jdn.co.il/j_world/2474197/)
- Trial Reels provide per-Reel test metrics (views, likes, comments, shares) after about 24 hours, usable as a "double down" signal before broad release. — [Instagram Creators blog](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels); [Vista Social](https://vistasocial.com/insights/instagram-trial-reels/)

### Inferences
- **Workflow**: export one clean master file per video with no platform watermark. Upload it natively to each platform, or use a scheduler that posts the original file (Metricool, Buffer and Later all support Reels, Shorts and TikTok). Never download from TikTok and re-upload with a watermark. Repurpose.io-style pipelines are acceptable only if they pull the watermark-free file.
- **Seeding**: post the clip to the brand's WhatsApp Channel plus Status, then send it to friendly Breslov and חיזוק groups and to Telegram channels with a native Hebrew caption and a call to forward. Do not automate Status with bots.
- **Account setup**: one brand account (e.g., "משיבת נפש") under the same handle on IG, TikTok, YouTube and FB, fronted by a consistent narrator's face or voice. The bio carries the link (landing page or WhatsApp catalog for buying the book). This is inference, as no source on brand vs. personal account performance was found.
- **Doubling down (inferred thresholds, not platform-official)**: when a Reel's sends per reach clearly beats the account's median, or a Short's viewed rate goes above about 70%, make follow-up versions on the same theme or teaching and test variant hooks through Trial Reels.

### Gaps
- No sourced data was found on Israeli WhatsApp forwarding behavior (forward rates, group sizes) or on Instagram Collabs performance numbers.
- No authoritative source was found on whether schedulers (Metricool, Buffer, Later, Repurpose.io) reduce reach compared with native upload. Common creator lore says no if the file is clean, but this is unverified.
- No sources were found on video-to-book funnels (link in bio vs. WhatsApp Business catalog vs. landing page conversion) for the Israeli market.
- No sourced 2025–2026 benchmarks were found for posting frequency or best posting times in Israel.
