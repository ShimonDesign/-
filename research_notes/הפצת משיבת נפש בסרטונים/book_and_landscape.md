## 1. What is משיבת נפש: compiler, sources, structure, themes, and quotable passages

### Takeaway
משיבת נפש is a short anthology of Rebbe Nachman's and Rebbe Nosson's teachings on inner strength (התחזקות), faith, teshuvah, joy and not despairing. Rabbi Moshe Yehoshua Bezhilianski (ר' אלתר מטעפליק) compiled it, and it first came out in Lemberg in 1902 (תרס"ב). Its whole point is "never despair", which makes it naturally suited to one-message short videos.

### Cited Findings
- The compiler was Rabbi Moshe Yehoshua Bezhilianski, known as ר' אלתר מטעפליק. He was a student of Rabbi Nachman of Tcherin and learned from other students of Rebbe Nosson. He joined the Breslov group in Uman after his second marriage, to the sister of Rabbi Avraham Chazan. — [Hamichlol, "רבי משה יהושע בזשיליאנסקי"](https://www.hamichlol.org.il/%D7%90%D7%9C%D7%AA%D7%A8_%D7%9E%D7%98%D7%A2%D7%A4%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%A7)
- His best-known books are **משיבת נפש** (Lemberg, תרס"ב/1902), on faith, teshuvah, strengthening oneself and serving God, and **השתפכות הנפש** (Jerusalem, תרס"ד/1904), on prayer and התבודדות. — [Hamichlol](https://www.hamichlol.org.il/%D7%90%D7%9C%D7%AA%D7%A8_%D7%9E%D7%98%D7%A2%D7%A4%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%A7)
- Hamichlol calls him the first to print the writings of Rebbe Nachman and Rebbe Nosson arranged by topic. That makes משיבת נפש a pre-curated, thematic "best of", not a new commentary. — [Hamichlol](https://www.hamichlol.org.il/%D7%90%D7%9C%D7%AA%D7%A8_%D7%9E%D7%98%D7%A2%D7%A4%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%A7)
- **Conflicting first-edition data:** one English source says the sefer was "first published in Jerusalem about 1904". That probably confuses it with השתפכות הנפש. Hamichlol gives Lemberg 1902. — [search snippet via English Wikipedia "Alter Tepliker"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alter_Tepliker); [Hamichlol](https://www.hamichlol.org.il/%D7%90%D7%9C%D7%AA%D7%A8_%D7%9E%D7%98%D7%A2%D7%A4%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%A7)
- The English edition is **"Restore My Soul"**, translated by Avraham Greenbaum and published by the Breslov Research Institute (ISBN 9781928822950). BRI describes it as Rebbe Nachman's teachings on fighting sadness and frustration "by tapping into the spiritual wellsprings of joy", and as helping readers put "hopelessness and despair aside". — [BRI books](https://books.breslov.org/?p=3539); [usbooks.breslov.org](https://usbooks.breslov.org/products/restore-my-soul); [buscalibre listing](https://buscalibre.cl/libro-restore-my-soul-meshivat-nefesh/9781928822950/p/53038256); [breslev.com product](https://breslev.com/product/restore-my-soul/)
- The introduction (seen in an English PDF rendering on nanach.org) tells the reader **not to despair under any circumstances, whatever he is going through**. This is the book's thesis line. — [nanach.org PDF "meshivas nefesh"](https://mail.nanach.org/books/meshivas-nefesh.pdf)
- Breslov distributors group משיבת נפש with the "books of the students" (ספרי התלמידים), next to Rebbe Nosson's works. Examples are the Breslov app "ברסלבוק" and the non-profit "מלא וגדיש". — [INN forum, ברסלבוק](https://www.inn.co.il/forum/t1407408); [JDN consumer article](https://www.jdn.co.il/consumer/2466289/)
- In Breslov outreach practice, the book is handed out together with "אין יאוש" pamphlets. A Hidabroot first-person piece describes reading from משיבת נפש during street outreach with "אין יאוש" booklets. — [Hidabroot, "שניות אור"](https://www.hidabroot.org/article/1126097)
- "אין שום ייאוש בעולם כלל" is treated as a deliberately provocative, radical-hope statement, not a neutral one. — [asif.co.il PDF essay](https://asif.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/27-36.pdf). Rebbe Nosson wrote to his son that if the world knew this, they would already merit the redemption (עלים לתרופה, letter רסט). — [INN, "3 נקודות אור מרבי נחמן"](https://www.inn.co.il/news/452884)
- "מצוה גדולה להיות בשמחה תמיד, ולהתגבר להרחיק העצבות והמרה שחרה בכל כחו… רק בשעה מיוחדת יהיה לו לב נשבר" comes from Likutei Moharan II:24. — [Kipa](https://www.kipa.co.il/יהדות/חגים/פורים/%d7%9e%d7%a8%d7%91%d7%99%d7%9f-%d7%91%d7%a9%d7%9e%d7%97%d7%94-%d7%91%d7%a9%d7%9e%d7%97%d7%94-%d7%aa%d7%9e%d7%99%d7%93/); [Wikiyeshiva, ליקוטי מוהר"ן](https://www.yeshiva.org.il/wiki/index.php/ליקוטי_מוהר%22ן)
- Likutei Moharan II:112 warns that humility must not turn into "terrible despair". A Steinsaltz Center summary adds the line "despair is the product of laziness; if you exert effort, you will find [your way]". — [Steinsaltz Center](https://steinsaltz-center.org/portal/library/Concise_Guide/Makshava/Torah%20Concepts/title/113/491)

### Inferences
- Because Alter of Teplik already arranged the material into short passages by topic, the book can be "chunked" almost directly into one passage, one video. The despair/התחזקות core gives a natural series spine ("35 days without despair").
- The safest primary hooks are #1, #3, #4, #5, #7 and #14. They are short, widely known and easy to verify. The famous paraphrases (#5's song wording, #27) should be labelled "בהשראת" ("inspired by") or quoted in their original wording, to avoid criticism from knowledgeable viewers.

## 2. Likely audience in Israel and which themes resonate today

### Takeaway
Rebbe Nachman is a cross-sector figure in Israel: car stickers, "נ נח", the Rosh Hashanah pilgrimage to Uman, and pop songs (Ishay Ribo, Shuli Rand and others). The "despair / bridge / joy" themes fit the post–October 7 mood of exhaustion, grief and reservist burnout. I found no source that directly measures Breslov content use among reservists.

### Cited Findings
- Rebbe Nachman is part of Israeli mainstream culture. Mainstream singers (Shuli Rand, Adi Ran, Aharon Razel, Yosef Karduner, Yisrael Dagan, Avraham Abutbul) recorded a tribute marking 200 years since his passing. — [Mako music](https://www.mako.co.il/music-news/local/article-f44f7447b71da21006.htm)
- Breslov Hasidim are visible on Israeli streets with cars carrying stickers and his sayings. Kan made a documentary on a Breslover who fought cancer through joy, an example of the "joy in suffering" narrative. — [Kan Dig](https://www.kan.org.il/content/dig/digital/p-11685/s2018/85542/)
- Reservists after October 7: INSS warns that the spirit and home-front support behind reserve service "may gradually fade". Much of the force mobilized on October 7 faced "prolonged and wearing service". — [INSS, מערך המילואים](https://www.inss.org.il/he/publication/reserve-october-7/); [INSS summary PDF](https://www.inss.org.il/he/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2025/07/סיכום.pdf)
- A Kan report covers reservist burnout ("Whatever they do, I can't go on any more"). I read only the snippet. — [Kan News](https://www.kan.org.il/content/kan-news/newstv/p-11894/s1/825226/)
- There is a cross-sector thirst for Jewish content online: a Srugim piece describes the growing phenomenon of "rabbaniyot" with Instagram and TikTok accounts posting strengthening (חיזוק) clips. An AI-generated "Rabbanit Gili" persona drew hundreds of thousands of followers, mostly women, and the article claims the thirst for Torah content "crosses sectors". — [Srugim](https://www.srugim.co.il/1190339-רבניות-רשת-באינסטגרם-תופעה-שהפכה-לפאר)
- Breslov street outreach deliberately targets secular and affluent neighborhoods with "אין יאוש" pamphlets and משיבת נפש. — [Hidabroot](https://www.hidabroot.org/article/1126097)

### Inferences
Audience segments, ranked by fit for משיבת נפש short video:
1. **Secular and traditional "seekers" (מסורתיים, חילונים מחפשים)**: they know "גשר צר מאד" from songs and stickers, and respond to universal wellbeing framing (anxiety, present moment, self-worth, talking to God in your own words).
2. **People in personal crisis** (divorce, illness, debt, addiction, depression): "אין שום ייאוש", "אם אתה מאמין שיכולין לקלקל" and "הנקודה הטובה".
3. **Post–October 7: bereaved families, released hostages' circles, reservists and their spouses**: courage (גשר צר), "joy is a battle", לב נשבר for one hour a day as healthy grieving time, "הירידה לצורך העלייה". Tone must stay gentle: never "be happy" aimed at the bereaved, and no triumphalism.
4. **Religious Zionist and Haredi Breslov core**: already consumers. They share via WhatsApp and are an amplification channel, not the growth audience.
- The themes most likely to resonate in 2026 are not despairing, fear and anxiety, starting over, self-compassion, simple talks with God (התבודדות as a "journaling/meditation" analog), and joy as resistance.

### Gaps
- I found no survey data on how many Israelis identify with or consume Breslov content, and no study of Breslov content among reservists or the bereaved. This needs separate research.

## 3. Who already does Breslov / Jewish-inspiration short video in Hebrew, and the book's publishers and prices

### Takeaway
The Hebrew Jewish-inspiration short-video space is crowded and growing: rabbis, "rabbaniyot", parasha creators, viral Selichot clips and personas like the Yanuka. Breslov institutions (Rabbi Shalom Arush's breslev.co.il / Chut Shel Chessed, BRI, Erez Moshe Doron) publish mostly long-form or lecture-clip content. I could not retrieve verified follower or view numbers for specific Breslov accounts. The book itself is cheap and sold through low-margin Breslov distribution, so the call to action should be "get it / learn it", not a heavy sales funnel.

### Cited Findings
- **breslev.com / breslev.co.il (Rabbi Shalom Arush)** keeps YouTube and TikTok presences, with clips of Rabbi Shalom Arush and Rabbi Nachman Arush. — [breslev.com](https://breslev.com/)
- **breslov.org (BRI)** publishes weekly video talks (Meir Elkabas, Chaim Kramer, Nasan Maimon) and animated versions of Rebbe Nachman's stories, mostly in English. — [breslov.org Watch](https://breslov.org/post-category/watch/); [Meir Elkabas 2026](https://breslov.org/meir-elkabas-parshat-lekh-lekha-2026/)
- **Rabbi Erez Moshe Doron** (born 1962) is a spiritual teacher whose teaching is based on Rebbe Nachman's writings. His lessons appear mainly on his personal YouTube channel. — [Hamichlol](https://www.hamichlol.org.il/%D7%90%D7%A8%D7%96_%D7%9E%D7%A9%D7%94_%D7%93%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%9F)
- **Rabbi Shlomo Yehuda Be'eri ("the Yanuka")** is "especially popular on TikTok" and described as the "hottest rockstar" of the admor/baba scene. Mako (N12) published a critical piece in Q4 2025. — [Mako N12](https://www.mako.co.il/news-israel/2025_q4/Article-e614fb8a3998a91027.htm)
- **Noam Yaakobson** makes short weekly-parasha videos with "impressive reach". He reports tens of thousands of views soon after posting and notes that WhatsApp-group sharing is not counted in the views, which shows how much hidden reach WhatsApp has. — [Makor Rishon](https://www.makorrishon.co.il/judaism/750303/)
- **"The Torah world came to TikTok"**: Makor Rishon covers celebrities, a secular creator and an American creator, and rabbis who "understood we must be there". — [Makor Rishon](https://www.makorrishon.co.il/culture/article/288362)
- **Selichot clips go viral on TikTok**: four creators regularly post footage from Selichot at synagogues and the Kotel, a format of authentic emotional moments with music. — [Maariv](https://www.maariv.co.il/news/israel/article-1357373)
- **Rabbaniyot on Instagram and TikTok** post strengthening clips and lesson excerpts. The AI persona "Rabbanit Gili" drew hundreds of thousands of followers. — [Srugim](https://www.srugim.co.il/1190339-רבניות-רשת-באינסטגרם-תופעה-שהפכה-לפאר)
- **Channel 2000** runs an AI series "בינת הצדיקים" with Tanakh and tzaddik stories in AI form, so AI depictions of tzaddikim already exist in the religious-media space. — [search result, JDN/Channel 2000 listing](https://www.jdn.co.il/music/2481396/)
- For scale benchmarks, Israeli news TikTok pages have about 130k to 350k followers (Keshet 12: 129.7k; Hadashot 12: 348.9k). — [ice.co.il](https://www.ice.co.il/social/news/article/1005589)
- **Book availability and price:**
  - The non-profit **"מלא וגדיש"** distributes Rebbe Nosson's books, including משיבת נפש, at distribution cost, explicitly without marketing promotions. It says a small book or "kuntres" can cost about ₪5. — [JDN consumer](https://www.jdn.co.il/consumer/2466289/)
  - Pocket editions are sold in Breslov shops in Mea Shearim and Bnei Brak (Nekuda Tova) and at Breslov stalls in Jerusalem, such as above the Kotel and at the Central Bus Station, according to forum users. — [INN forum](https://www.inn.co.il/forum/t966891)

  - The English edition is "Restore My Soul" from BRI. — [usbooks.breslov.org](https://usbooks.breslov.org/products/restore-my-soul); [breslev.com](https://breslev.com/product/restore-my-soul/)

### Inferences
- Formats that work in this space: (a) a talking-head rabbi giving a 30–45s "חיזוק" with captions, (b) authentic emotional moments with niggun (Selichot, Uman), (c) text-on-screen quotes over nature or b-roll with music, (d) personal-story testimony ("I was at the bottom…"), (e) AI personas, which attract followers but also criticism.
- A gap in the market: a **book-anchored series** (one משיבת נפש passage per day, numbered) with a consistent visual identity. No source I found shows anyone doing this specifically for משיבת נפש.
- Call to action: since the book costs only a few shekels or is free in an app, the realistic CTA is "download / get a pocket copy / join the WhatsApp channel for the daily passage". Profit-focused selling would clash with Breslov distribution norms ("מלא וגדיש": no promotions).
- WhatsApp sharing is the hidden multiplier (Yaakobson). Design clips to be "forwardable": vertical, captioned, without platform watermarks.

## 4. Sensitivities: halachic and community norms, and copyright

### Takeaway
The biggest risks are a fabricated "portrait" of Rebbe Nachman (AI or otherwise), female singing voice for Haredi viewers, modesty of imagery, posting on Shabbat, and misattributed or misworded quotes. Copyright on the 1902 text itself is very likely expired, but modern editions, typesetting and translations (for example BRI's English) are protected.

### Cited Findings
- **Female singing voice (קול באשה):** the prohibition stems from Berachot 24a ("קול באשה ערווה"). Some authorities (Chelkat Yaakov) prohibit even recorded singing. Others (Maharam Shik, and some modern poskim) are lenient for recordings or unknown women, and video may be stricter than audio for some. — [Etzion, Deracheha on Kol Isha](https://etzion.org.il/sites/default/files/2024-11/55deracheha-h-%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%9C-%D7%90%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%94-3.pdf); [Yeshiva.org.il](https://www.yeshiva.org.il/midrash/1831)
- **AI and religious authority:** Haredi rabbis published letters warning about "the terrible danger" of OpenAI-type services, and a NY rabbinic statement called AI an "abomination". An Israeli centre warned that AI rabbi personas can mislead and called for disclosure labels. — [JDN](https://www.jdn.co.il/j_world/j_news/1953827/); [Ynetnews](https://www.ynetnews.com/jewish-world/article/bkwauiwv3); [Harvard Berkman, June 2026](https://cyber.harvard.edu/story/2026-06/ai-reshapes-society-jewish-leaders-grapple-what-comes-next)
- AI tzaddik content already exists (Channel 2000's "בינת הצדיקים"), so it is not unheard of. Still, it is contested. — [JDN listing](https://www.jdn.co.il/music/2481396/)

### Inferences
- **No image of Rebbe Nachman.** **[TK]** There is no authentic portrait, and Breslov circles regard invented likenesses as inappropriate. Use his chair (in Jerusalem), the Uman tziyun, handwriting, manuscripts, or text/typography instead. I found no explicit Breslov ruling in search, so treat this as a strong community norm, not a documented psak.
- **Imagery:** avoid women's faces and bodies if targeting Haredi and Breslov sharing networks; use landscapes, hands, silhouettes, men in prayer, the sea, a bridge. For secular audiences, modest, mixed imagery is fine, but one set of assets that works for all sectors maximizes WhatsApp forwarding.
- **Voice:** a male narrator, or text plus niggun (male voice or instrumental), is the safe default. Offer a separate women's-only track or channel if desired.
- **Shabbat and Yom Tov:** do not schedule posts between candle lighting and havdalah (Israel time). Scheduled posting is debated, and many religious creators avoid it because of מראית עין and "עובדין דחול". Also be careful around Yom HaZikaron and Tisha B'Av tone.
- **Quote accuracy:** knowledgeable Breslov viewers will call out misquotes such as "העיקר לא לפחד כלל" vs the original "שלא יתפחד כלל". Show the source on screen (e.g., "ליקוטי מוהר"ן ח"ב מח / משיבת נפש").
- **Copyright:** the original 1902 compilation is almost certainly public domain, since the author died about a century ago (**[TK]** 1919). Modern Hebrew editions with new typesetting, vowels, punctuation or notes, and translations like BRI's "Restore My Soul", are protected. Quote from the original text, retype it, and do not reproduce scans or modern annotations without permission. Some Breslov publishers print a "חרם/הסכמה" against copying. Check whatever edition is used.
- **Tone with trauma:** avoid "everything is for the good", which can hurt the bereaved. Frame Rebbe Nachman's joy as a choice and a fight, and his לב נשבר as legitimate.
